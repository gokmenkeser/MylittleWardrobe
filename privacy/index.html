My Little Wardrobe

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. About this policy

This policy covers My Little Wardrobe, the iOS and Android dress-up game developed by Gökmen Keser, and this privacy page. Contact: gokmenkeser@hotmail.com. Last updated: 13 September 2026.

2. Information saved on your device

The game saves character and clothing selections, favorites, progress, reward dates, language, sound and music preferences, outfit album images, and a cached Premium entitlement in the app's local storage. Album pictures are generated from the game scene, not your camera or personal photo library. These gameplay files are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. Device or operating-system backups may include them depending on your settings.

3. Purchases and store services

Optional Premium is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments through Unity In-App Purchasing. The app processes product identifiers, purchase/receipt or transaction information, and ownership status as needed to grant and restore Premium. It may contact the store when the game starts or resumes, not only when you press Buy. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details. Store account, payment, fraud-prevention and diagnostic processing is governed by the relevant provider's policies.

4. Accounts, advertising and permissions

The game does not require a developer account and does not include advertising, chat, public outfit uploads or online leaderboards. No separate gameplay analytics service is integrated in the current version. The game does not request access to your contacts, precise location, microphone or camera. Platform services may process technical information independently of the local gameplay data described above.

5. Children and parents

My Little Wardrobe is designed for children and families. We do not ask children to enter their name, email address or other personal details in the game. A parental gate precedes access to purchases; it is not an age-verification service or a substitute for parental consent where required. Support enquiries should be sent by a parent or guardian. Please do not send a child's personal details or real photographs. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

6. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name shown by your email account, the message and attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, diagnose problems and handle purchase or privacy enquiries. Email is handled through our Microsoft-hosted mailbox. Please do not send passwords, card details or unnecessary personal information. If purchase proof is needed, redact unrelated details.

7. Sharing, security and international processing

We do not sell personal information or use support messages for targeted advertising. Information may be handled by the store, email and website-hosting providers to deliver their services, or disclosed when legally required. Providers may process information in countries other than yours under their applicable terms and safeguards. We use access controls and reasonable precautions, but no storage or transmission system is completely secure.

8. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data remains until removed by you or your device. On Android you can clear app storage; on iOS deleting the app normally removes local app data, while offloading may retain it. Backups can retain or restore data separately. Removing local data does not erase store purchase records or cancel your Premium ownership. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the enquiry and meet legitimate security or legal needs. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold; rights and any legally required retention depend on applicable law. We cannot remotely access or delete your local game files or store account records.

9. This website

This privacy page does not include advertising or analytics scripts and does not set cookies or use browser local storage. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure the site. External links and email services have their own privacy practices.

10. Providers and updates

See Apple Privacy, Google Privacy, Unity Privacy, Microsoft Privacy and, when hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub Privacy. We may update this policy as the game or its services change and will revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated as required. This policy does not replace applicable statutory rights.
