My Little Wardrobe

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. About this policy

This policy covers My Little Wardrobe, the iOS and Android dress-up game developed by Gökmen Keser, and this privacy page. For privacy or support enquiries, contact gokmenkeser@hotmail.com.

2. Information saved on your device

The game saves character and clothing selections, favorites, progress, reward dates, language, sound and music preferences, outfit album images, and a locally cached Premium entitlement in the app's local storage.

Outfit album images are generated from the game scene. They do not come from your camera or personal photo library. These gameplay files are not uploaded to a server operated by the developer. Device or operating-system backups may include local game data depending on your device and backup settings.

3. Purchases and store services

Premium is an optional, one-time, non-consumable purchase. Apple App Store or Google Play processes the payment through Unity In-App Purchasing. There are no subscriptions or recurring payments.

The app processes product identifiers, store receipts or transaction information, and entitlement status when necessary to display products, complete purchases, permanently unlock Premium content, and restore previous purchases. The app may contact the relevant store when it starts or resumes, not only when the Buy button is pressed.

The developer does not receive or store full payment-card details. Store account, payment, fraud-prevention, purchase-record and related processing is governed by Apple, Google and their applicable policies.

4. Technical diagnostics and performance data

Unity In-App Purchasing may process limited app diagnostics and app performance information to provide and maintain purchasing functionality. According to Unity's documentation for the version used by the game, this technical information is processed transiently, is required for app functionality, and is not used by the developer for advertising, profiling or behavioral analytics.

The game does not include a separate gameplay analytics service. We do not use this technical information to identify children, create user profiles, personalize advertising or track users across apps or websites.

5. Accounts, advertising and permissions

The game does not require a developer-operated user account and does not include advertising, chat, public outfit uploads or online leaderboards.

The game does not request access to your contacts, precise location, microphone or camera. Platform and store services may process technical or transaction information independently under their own privacy policies.

6. Children and parents

My Little Wardrobe is designed for children and families. We do not ask children to enter their name, email address, phone number or other personal details in the game.

A parental gate is presented before access to purchasing features. The parental gate is not an age-verification service and is not a substitute for parental consent where consent is legally required.

Support enquiries should be sent by a parent or guardian. Please do not send a child's personal details or real photographs. If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

7. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name shown by your email account, and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use this information only to respond, diagnose problems, and handle purchase, support or privacy enquiries.

Email is handled through our Microsoft-hosted mailbox. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unnecessary personal information. If purchase proof is needed, please redact unrelated personal and payment details.

8. Sharing, security and international processing

We do not sell personal information, use support messages for targeted advertising, or share local gameplay data with advertising companies.

Information may be processed by app-store, purchasing, email and website-hosting providers when necessary to provide their services, or disclosed when legally required. These providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable terms and safeguards.

We use reasonable access controls and security precautions, but no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed by you or your device. On Android, you can clear the app's storage. On iOS, deleting the app normally removes local app data, while offloading the app may retain it. Device backups may retain or restore data separately.

Removing local data does not erase an app-store purchase record or cancel Premium ownership. Store purchase records are controlled by Apple or Google.

Transient technical diagnostic and performance data is not maintained by the developer as a user account or persistent user profile.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the enquiry and meet legitimate security, accounting or legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information that we hold. We cannot remotely access or delete local game files stored only on your device or records controlled by your app-store account.

10. Offline use

Core dress-up features can be used offline after the game and its content have been installed. An internet connection is required to retrieve store information, make a purchase, verify entitlement or restore a previous purchase.

11. This website

This privacy page does not include advertising or analytics scripts and does not set cookies or use browser local storage. The language buttons change only the visible text on this page and do not save your selection.

The hosting provider may process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the website. External links and email services have their own privacy practices.

12. Providers and policy updates

You can learn more from Apple Privacy, Google Privacy, Unity Privacy, Microsoft Privacy and GitHub Privacy.

We may update this policy when the game, its purchasing system or its service providers change. We will update the date at the top of this page and communicate material changes where required. This policy does not replace rights provided by applicable law.
